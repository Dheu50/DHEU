# REGRET

This was not my expectation,

 When we had our first collision. 

Our eyes met each other for a second,

 And we walked past so quickly like it was another mission. 

We had a few conversations; 

You held me up when I was falling apart. 

But just like a new season, 

I saw you with another one.

No, I don't write diaries, 

But I had a lot to say:

"I could have stopped him and declared how he made me feel safe, 

Or maybe the feelings I hide deep inside. 

Can we not be like another stranger when we encounter each other next time?"

I think sometimes if I could be brave, 

I could at least have one true friend.

 He could be my sweet and sour mix; 

I could be his clumsy little pumpkin.

 But it was all in my imagination; 

A life so dark could not get brightened up by one. 

I lost him for good, and I know.

That's the secret regret making my heart now hollow.
