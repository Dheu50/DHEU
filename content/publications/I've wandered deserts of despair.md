I’ve wandered deserts of despair,

With blistered feet and soot-stained air,

Yet never begged the sky for grace

Just watched who walked at my own pace.



So if you see no medals here,

No laurels carved in loud veneer,

Know that your trust—so softly cast—

Outshines the triumphs of my past.



I may not wear the victor’s hue,

But still, I rise. And this

this verse—is all for you.
