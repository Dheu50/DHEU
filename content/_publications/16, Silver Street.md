16, Silver Street. A very ordinary looking modern house with dull colors and sharp angles, gray being the major color in the scheme. Boring in every way. The rooms were sharp cuboids, and both floors had white marble floors and plain white putty ceilings. The windows were placed well though, the sunlight fell on the hallways and rooms both at sunrise and sunset. The very definition of 'well lit'.



The owner, Venessa Muller, was a painter, who spent most of her time in her little studio- a room in the second floor of her house with shelves filled to the brim with painting and drawing supplies: brushes, colors of every kind, canvases, pencils, charcoal, her well used color palette placed on the table, longingly waiting for her arrival. People love her paintings, she's made a lot of money by selling some of her best ones, though, she doesn’t really sell much of her work…



Oh, and Venessa's favorite in her house, her basement, where she has kept most of her paintings on the ample available walls, which also stores her car, which is not luxurious, but does the job...



Venessa is cooking right now, another one of her hobbies. She loves cooking... but hates that she has to cook for two people, one portion for her, one for June. Oh, who's June?



June is Venessa's best friend. Oh, is June coming over today? Oh, no, no. She's been here for a long time. What do I mean? Let's wait until Venessa visits the basement today after two days…



"Hey there June..." Venessa says cheerfully.



"!!" replied the duct taped figure.



"Food!" Venessa said, then she tore away the duct tape from June's lips.



"Why are you doing this, Erica?!"



"Shh, Erica isn't here, June, you should save your voice, it's of no use..."



Tears flood June's eyes. Venessa gently wipes them away and whispers "Shh, now eat, this is delicious…”
