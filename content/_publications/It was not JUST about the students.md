It was not JUST about the students, friends.

1. The trans people act mandates medical screening, bureaucratic gatekeeping, and compulsory disclosure of intimate health details infringe upon the fundamental right to privacy and bodily autonomy.

2. Over 1 crore vacancies across central government jobs are yet to be filled.

3. Unemployment is highest since independence.

4. In 2014-15, India's imports from China were approximately $60.4 billion. Currently, India's imports have surged to over $131.5 billion, making China India's largest trading partner.

5. Inflation is at an all time high.

6. Indigenous lands are being auctioned to the rich in MP, Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh, Assam, Nicobar, for mining and building resorts and what not.

7. At least 18-19 mothers died in Rajasthan shortly after giving birth in the last 3 months.

8. We've been paying a premium on petrol and diesel for years when it was cheap. The companies benefited. Now the prices have been raised while they sell third grade ethanol mixed fuel that's damaging our vehicles.

9. On the Henley Passport Index, India moved from 76th in 2014 to 81st in 2026.

10. India is ranked second from the bottom at 176th out of 177 countries in the global Environment Performance Index.

11. India ranks 102nd out of 123 countries in the latest Global Hunger Index. We trail behind Nepal, Sri Lanka, and Bangladesh.

12. India ranks 157th out of 180 countries in the RSF World Press Freedom Index.

I can go on and on about the issues. The fight is FOR INDIA. To save it from the people who're tarnishing the legacy of our great motherland.
